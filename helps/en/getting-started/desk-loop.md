# Clip

Capture text from other apps, draft outbound notes, and check them before you paste them out. The `.compass` folder stays hidden in Explorer — open **Clip** from the left sidebar, or **View → Show Clip** (`Ctrl+Shift+D`).

## Quick path

1. Copy text anywhere
2. Hotkey (default: `Ctrl+Alt+I`) saves it to inbox
3. Open Clip → **Create draft…** (mail / minutes / report / chat) and Apply the AI preview
4. In Outbox, use **Check & copy**, then paste into your mailer
5. When finished, **Done** (archive / hide from list) or **Delete**
6. Optionally **Create digest** for the last 7 days

## Settings

Settings → **Clip** controls the hotkey and what opens after capture.
